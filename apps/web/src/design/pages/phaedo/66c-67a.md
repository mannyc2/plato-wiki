## What’s happening {#happening}

The speech Socrates gives the lovers of wisdom goes on. Diseases of the body hinder their pursuit of the truth,[^disease] and the body fills them with passions, desires and fears, so that it really and truly makes it impossible for them to think at all.[^think] The body and its desires, they say, are the only cause of wars and factions and battles,[^wars] since all wars arise for the sake of money, and money must be gained for the sake of the body.[^money] Even when they get a little leisure for philosophy, the body keeps breaking in with noise and confusion.[^noise] If they are ever to know anything absolutely, they must be free from the body and behold the realities with the eye of the soul alone.[^eye] So if pure knowledge is impossible while the body is with them, either it cannot be acquired at all or only when they are dead,[^either] for only then will the soul be by itself, apart from the body.[^apart] While they live, they will, they think, be nearest to knowledge if they avoid communion with the body so far as possible,[^nearest] and keep pure from it until God himself sets them free.[^free] Freed in this way and pure, they will, they think, be with the pure and know of themselves all that is pure.[^withpure]
