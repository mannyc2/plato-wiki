## What’s happening {#happening}

Crito protests that the sun is still upon the mountains and has not yet set,[^sun] and that others have taken the poison very late, after eating and drinking: there is still time.[^time] Socrates answers that those men are right to do as they do, since they think they gain by it,[^right] but he thinks he would gain nothing by taking the poison a little later;[^gain] he would only make himself ridiculous in his own eyes by clinging to life.[^ridiculous] Crito nods to the boy standing near,[^boy] who comes back with the man who is to give the poison, carrying it in a cup.[^cup] The man tells Socrates to drink it and walk about till his legs feel heavy, then lie down.[^legs] Socrates took the cup, Phaedo tells Echecrates, very gently, without trembling or changing color or expression.[^gently] He asks whether he may pour a libation to some deity from the cup,[^libation] and the man says they prepare only as much as they think is enough.[^enough]
