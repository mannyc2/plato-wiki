## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates believes that those who, like him, live between the pillars of Hercules and the river Phasis dwell in a small part of the earth about the sea, like ants or frogs about a pond,[^ants] and that many other people live in other such regions.[^others] The earth has many hollows, he says, into which water and mist and air have run together,[^hollows] but the earth itself is pure and lies in the pure heaven where the stars are.[^pure] We do not perceive, he says, that we live in the hollows, but think we live on the upper surface of the earth,[^surface] like someone living in the depth of the ocean who sees the sun and stars through the water and thinks the sea is the sky.[^sea] If anyone could come to the top of the air or fly up, Socrates says, he would see that upper world as fishes lifting their heads out of the water see ours,[^fishes] and, if his nature could bear the sight, he would recognize the real heaven and the real light and the real earth.[^real] This earth of ours, he says, with its stones, is corroded, as things in the sea are corroded by the brine,[^brine] and the things in that world above would be seen to be even more superior to ours.[^superior]
