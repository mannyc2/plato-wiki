## What’s happening {#happening}

In Socrates’ story, the rivers all flow back into Tartarus below the point where they came out,[^below] some coiling about the earth like serpents first.[^serpents] Among them are four great streams. Oceanus, the greatest and outermost, flows round in a circle;[^oceanus] opposite it, flowing the other way, is Acheron,[^acheron] which passes under the earth to the Acherusian lake.[^lake] To that lake the souls of most of the dead go,[^dead] and after the appointed time they are sent back to be born again into living beings.[^born] The third river falls into a vast region burning with a great fire[^fire] and makes a lake boiling with water and mud;[^boiling] it is called Pyriphlegethon, and the streams of lava that spout up on earth are offshoots from it.[^lava] The fourth issues, it is said, into a wild and awful place, all of a dark blue color,[^blue] and the lake it forms there is the Styx;[^styx] the name of this river, as the poets say, is Cocytus.[^cocytus]
