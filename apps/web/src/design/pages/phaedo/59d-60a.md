## What’s happening {#happening}

From here Phaedo tells Echecrates what happened in the prison. On the earlier days, he says, the friends used to meet at daybreak in the court where the trial took place, near the prison,[^court] and then spent most of the day with Socrates.[^day] The evening before, they heard that the ship had arrived from Delos,[^ship] so they agreed to come as early in the morning as possible.[^early] The jailer who usually answered the door told them to wait, because, he said, the Eleven were releasing Socrates from his fetters and giving directions how he was to die that day.[^eleven] Inside they found Socrates just released from his fetters,[^released] and Xanthippe sitting beside him with his little son in her arms.[^son] When she saw them, Xanthippe cried out that this was the last time his friends would speak to him, or he to them.[^last] Socrates asked Crito to have somebody take her home,[^home] and some of Crito’s people led her away wailing.[^wailing]
