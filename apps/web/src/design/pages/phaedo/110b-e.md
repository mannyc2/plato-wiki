## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates offers a story, if he may tell one, about the things on the earth below the heaven,[^story] and Simmias would be glad to hear it.[^glad] Seen from above, the earth is said to look like one of those balls covered with twelve pieces of leather,[^ball] divided into patches of color of which ours may be regarded as samples, such as painters use.[^samples] There, Socrates says, the whole earth is of such colors, much brighter and purer than ours:[^brighter] one part purple of wonderful beauty, one golden, one whiter than chalk or snow.[^colors] The things that grow there are correspondingly beautiful, and the mountains and stones are smoother, more transparent and more lovely in color than ours.[^stones] Our prized jaspers and emeralds and other gems, he says, are fragments of those stones,[^fragments] which are pure, not corroded or defiled with filth and brine as ours are.[^pure]
