## What’s happening {#happening}

Simmias holds to what they agreed before: he is persuaded he accepted it on sufficient and right grounds,[^grounds] so he cannot accept, from himself or anyone else, that the soul is a harmony.[^harmony] Socrates offers him another way of looking at it,[^another] and Simmias grants each step.

:::moves
1. A harmony can neither do nor suffer anything other than the elements it is composed of.[^suffer]
2. So it cannot lead those elements, only follow them,[^follow] and can do nothing opposed to its own parts.[^opposed]
3. A harmony would be more completely a harmony the more fully it is harmonized, assuming that to be possible.[^more]
4. But no soul is in the slightest degree more a soul than another.[^soul]
5. One soul is said to have sense and virtue and to be good, another folly and wickedness and to be bad,[^good] and Simmias agrees that this is true.[^true]
:::

Socrates asks what those who take the soul for a harmony will say virtue and wickedness are: another harmony inside the harmony, and a discord?[^discord] Simmias cannot tell, though he thinks they would say something of that sort.[^sort]
