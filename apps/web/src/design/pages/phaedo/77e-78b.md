## What’s happening {#happening}

Cebes laughs: perhaps it is not they who are afraid, but a child within them who has such fears,[^child] and that child should be persuaded not to fear death as if it were a hobgoblin.[^hobgoblin] Socrates says they must sing charms to him every day until they charm his fear away.[^charms] Where will they find a good singer of such charms, Cebes asks, since Socrates is leaving them?[^leaving] Socrates tells him that Hellas is large and there are many foreign peoples too, and they should search through all of them, sparing neither money nor toil,[^search] and search among themselves as well, since they might hardly find others better able to do it.[^yourselves] Cebes promises it will be done and asks to return to the argument.[^return] Socrates proposes that they ask what kind of thing naturally suffers dispersion and what kind does not, then inquire to which kind the soul belongs, and base their hopes or fears for their souls on the answer.[^class]
