## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates’ story goes underground. The regions are connected by many subterranean channels,[^channels] and there are everlasting rivers of huge size under the earth, of hot and cold water, rivers of fire, and many streams of mud.[^rivers] A kind of oscillation within the earth moves all these up and down.[^oscillation] One chasm is greater than the rest[^chasm] and is bored right through the whole earth;[^bored] Homer and many other poets, Socrates says, have called it Tartarus.[^tartarus] All the rivers flow together into it and out again.[^flow] Because this liquid has no bottom or foundation, it waves up and down,[^bottom] and the wind that follows it causes terrible and irresistible blasts as it rushes in and out.[^blasts] As the water moves to one region or the other, it fills the rivers there, which make seas and marshes and rivers and springs,[^seas] and then go down under the earth again.[^down]
