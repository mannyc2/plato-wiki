## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates explains that he composed them because he wished to test the meaning of certain dreams.[^dreams] The same dream came to him often in his past life,[^same] always telling him to make music and work at it.[^music] He used to think it was urging him on, as people cheer runners,[^runners] to do what he was already doing, since philosophy was the greatest kind of music.[^greatest] But after the trial, while the festival of the god delayed his execution,[^festival] he thought the dream might really mean what is ordinarily called music,[^ordinary] and that it was safer not to depart before making sure he had done what he ought.[^safer] So he first composed a hymn to the god whose festival it was;[^hymn] then, thinking that a poet must compose myths and not speeches,[^myths] and being no maker of myths himself, he put into verse those of Aesop’s myths he came upon first.[^aesop] He tells Cebes to bid Evenus farewell, and to tell him, if he is wise, to come after Socrates as quickly as he can.[^follow]
