## What’s happening {#happening}

In Socrates’ story, precious things lie in plain sight on that earth, abundant and large, so that it is a sight to make those blessed who look upon it.[^blessed] Animals and men live upon it, some on the coasts of the air, as we live about the sea;[^coasts] what water is to us, air is to them, and what air is to us, ether is to them.[^ether] Their seasons are so tempered that they have no diseases and live much longer than we do,[^diseases] and in sight and hearing and wisdom they are as far above us as air is purer than water.[^wisdom] They have temples in which the gods really dwell,[^temples] and they have intercourse with the gods by speech and prophecies and visions.[^visions] They see the sun and moon and stars as they really are.[^sun] Round about the whole earth, in its hollows, Socrates goes on, are many regions, some deeper and wider than the one we live in.[^regions]
