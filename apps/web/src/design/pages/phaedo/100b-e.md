## What’s happening {#happening}

What Socrates means is nothing new, he says.[^new] He will assume that there are such things as absolute beauty and good and greatness,[^assume] and if Cebes grants this, Socrates believes he can explain cause and prove that the soul is immortal.[^prove] Cebes grants it.[^grant] Then, Socrates says, if anything besides absolute beauty is beautiful, it is beautiful only because it partakes of absolute beauty,[^partakes] and Cebes accepts this view of cause.[^view] Socrates lets go of other causes such as color or shape,[^shape] and holds simply and plainly and perhaps foolishly[^plainly] that nothing makes a thing beautiful but the presence or communion of absolute beauty.[^presence] How that happens he does not yet say; he insists only that beautiful things are made beautiful by beauty.[^insist] He thinks this the safest answer he can give,[^safest] and in the same way great things are great by greatness, and smaller things smaller by smallness.[^greatness]
