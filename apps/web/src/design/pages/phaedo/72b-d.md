## What’s happening {#happening}

If generation did not go round from opposite to opposite and back, as in a circle, but always went forward in a straight line,[^line] Socrates argues, in the end all things would have the same form and stop being generated at all.[^stop] If there were falling asleep but no waking from sleep,[^waking] the sleeping Endymion would be mere nonsense,[^endymion] for everything else would be sound asleep as he is.[^asleep] If all things were mixed together and never separated, the saying of Anaxagoras would soon come true.[^anaxagoras] Just so, if all things that have life should die, and the dead should remain in that condition,[^remain] at last all things would be dead and nothing alive.[^dead] And if the living came from anything other than the dead, and the living were to die,[^other] Socrates asks, is there any escape from all things being swallowed up in death?[^swallowed] Cebes sees none,[^none] and says that what Socrates says seems to be perfectly true.[^true] Socrates thinks it is absolutely so, and that they are not deluded in making these admissions:[^deluded] the return to life is an actual fact, the living are generated from the dead, and the souls of the dead exist.[^exist]
