## What’s happening {#happening}

Cebes agrees that whatever becomes smaller must have been greater first,[^smaller] and Socrates adds that the weaker is generated from the stronger, and Cebes agrees.[^weaker] So they take it as sufficiently established that all things are generated in this way, opposites from opposites.[^established] Between each pair, Socrates goes on, there are two kinds of generation, from one to the other and back again,[^two] as with increasing and decreasing;[^increasing] even where we lack the words for it,[^words] there is always a process of generation from each to the other, and Cebes agrees.[^always] Socrates asks whether anything is the opposite of living, as being awake is the opposite of sleeping,[^opposite] and Cebes answers that being dead is.[^dead] Then, being opposites, these two are generated from each other, Socrates says, and the processes between them are two.[^processes] He takes the pair of sleeping and waking for himself and leaves life and death to Cebes:[^pair] being awake is generated from sleeping, and sleeping from being awake.[^sleep]
