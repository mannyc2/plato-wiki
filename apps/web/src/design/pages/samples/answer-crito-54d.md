## What’s happening {#happening}

The Laws have finished, and Socrates speaks as himself again.[^hear] Their words ring in him the way the flutes ring for Cybele’s worshippers, so loudly that he can hear nothing else.[^ring] He tells Crito that arguing against them now would be in vain, then asks him to speak if he can.[^vain]

[^hear]: crito 54d "this is what I seem to hear"
[^ring]: crito 54d "this sound of these words re-echoes within me and prevents my hearing any other words"
[^vain]: crito 54d "if you argue against these words you will speak in vain"
