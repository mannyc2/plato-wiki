## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates draws out what follows from Euthyphro’s own words, and Euthyphro grants each step.

:::moves
1. Some gods think some things right, noble and good, and other gods disagree,[^some] or they would not quarrel.[^quarrel]
2. Each group of gods loves what it thinks good and right, and hates the opposite.[^love]
3. So, it seems, the same things are both loved and hated by the gods, both dear to them and hateful.[^same]
4. So by this answer the same things would be both holy and unholy.[^both]
:::

To the last step Euthyphro answers, “I suppose so.”[^suppose] Then, Socrates says, Euthyphro has not answered his question, for Socrates did not ask what is at once holy and unholy.[^notask] It would not be surprising, Socrates adds, if Euthyphro’s punishment of his father pleased Zeus but was hateful to Cronus and Uranus.[^cronus] Euthyphro objects that, as he thinks, no god disagrees with any other on this point: whoever kills wrongfully ought to pay the penalty.[^penalty]

[^some]: euthyphro 7e "some of the gods too think some things are right or wrong and noble or disgraceful, and good or bad, and others disagree"
[^quarrel]: euthyphro 7e "they would not quarrel with each other if they did not disagree about these matters"
[^love]: euthyphro 7e "the gods in each group love the things which they consider good and right and hate the opposites of these things"
[^same]: euthyphro 8a "the same things would be dear and hateful to the gods"
[^both]: euthyphro 8a "And then the same things would be both holy and unholy, Euthyphro, according to this statement."
[^suppose]: euthyphro 8a "I suppose so."
[^notask]: euthyphro 8a "For I did not ask you what is at once holy and unholy"
[^cronus]: euthyphro 8b "it would not be surprising if, in punishing your father as you are doing, you were performing an act that is pleasing to Zeus, but hateful to Cronus and Uranus"
[^penalty]: euthyphro 8b "none of the gods disagrees with any other about this, or holds that he who kills anyone wrongfully ought not to pay the penalty"
