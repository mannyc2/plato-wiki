## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates puts a question: “Is that which is holy loved by the gods because it is holy, or is it holy because it is loved by the gods?”[^question] Euthyphro does not know what Socrates means,[^dontknow] so Socrates explains with pairs of words. He puts each step as a question, and Euthyphro agrees to each.

:::moves
1. Being carried and carrying, being led and leading, being seen and seeing differ from each other,[^pairs] and so do being loved and loving.[^loving]
2. A thing is a carried thing because someone carries it, not for some other reason.[^carried]
3. No one sees a thing because it is a seen thing; it is a seen thing because someone sees it.[^seen]
4. In general, what becomes or undergoes something is in that state because it becomes or undergoes it, not the other way round.[^undergo]
5. Being loved is a case of becoming or undergoing something.[^beloved]
6. So those who love a thing do not love it because it is a beloved thing; it is a beloved thing because they love it.[^because]
:::
