## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates spells out his question: is all that is holy right, but not all that is right holy, only part of it?[^part] Euthyphro cannot follow,[^follow] so Socrates explains by disagreeing with a poet’s line, that where fear is, there also is reverence.[^poet] Euthyphro agrees at each step.

:::moves
1. Many, it seems to Socrates, fear disease and poverty without revering what they fear; so reverence is not everywhere that fear is.[^disease]
2. But whoever feels reverence and shame about an act also fears a reputation for wickedness, so where reverence is, there is fear.[^shame]
3. Reverence is a part of fear, as the odd is a part of number.[^odd]
4. In the same way, holiness is a part of the right: where holiness is, there is the right, but not everywhere the right is.[^holypart] Euthyphro thinks this correct.[^agree]
5. Then, apparently, they must find out what part of the right holiness is,[^which] as Socrates could say what part of number the even is.[^even]
:::
