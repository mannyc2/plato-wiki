## What’s happening {#happening}

This time, Socrates says, Euthyphro has answered in the way he asked,[^asked] though whether the answer is true he is not yet sure.[^sure] Then he examines it, putting each step as a question, and Euthyphro agrees at every step.

:::moves
1. What is dear to the gods is holy, what is hateful to them is unholy,[^hateful] and the two are exact opposites.[^opposites]
2. It has also been said that the gods quarrel and disagree, and that there is enmity between them.[^quarrel]
3. A disagreement over which of two numbers is greater would not make two people enemies; they would settle it by arithmetic,[^arithmetic] as they would settle a question of weight by weighing.[^weighing]
4. The disagreements that make people enemies are about right and wrong, noble and disgraceful, good and bad,[^right] where they differ and cannot reach a satisfactory agreement.[^agreement]
5. If the gods disagree, they too disagree about these questions.[^gods]
:::
