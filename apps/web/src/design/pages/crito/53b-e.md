## What’s happening {#happening}

The Laws turn to what escape would bring. His friends would risk exile and the loss of their property.[^friends] In a well-governed city such as Thebes or Megara, he would arrive as an enemy of its laws,[^enemy] and confirm his judges in thinking their verdict just,[^verdict] since a man who destroys laws would surely be taken for a corrupter of the young.[^young] Could he go on telling people there that virtue, justice and the laws matter most?[^talk] In lawless Thessaly, people might be amused at how he slipped out of prison in disguise;[^disguise] and if he offended anyone, he would hear that an old man with little time left had clung so greedily to life that he broke the highest laws.[^greed]

[^enemy]: crito 53b "will go as an enemy, Socrates, to their government"
[^verdict]: crito 53c "you will confirm the judges in their opinion, so that they will think their verdict was just"
[^young]: crito 53c "he who is destroyer of the laws might certainly be regarded as a destroyer of young and thoughtless men"
[^talk]: crito 53c "saying that virtue and justice and lawful things and the laws are the most precious things to men"
[^disguise]: crito 53d "the ludicrous way in which you ran away from prison by putting on a disguise"
[^greed]: crito 53e "clung to life with such shameless greed that you transgressed the highest laws"
