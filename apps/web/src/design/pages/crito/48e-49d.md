## What’s happening {#happening}

Socrates wants to act with Crito’s approval, not against his wishes.[^approval] So he goes back to what they have long agreed: doing wrong is always bad and shameful for the one who does it,[^evil] so one must never do wrong, not even to repay a wrong, as most people think one may.[^requite] Nor may one harm anyone in return, whatever he has done, since harming people is the same as wronging them.[^harm] He warns Crito not to agree to something he does not believe.[^believe] Few people believe this, or ever will,[^few] and those who do and those who do not cannot reason together.[^common]

[^approval]: crito 48e "I am anxious to act in this matter with your approval, and not contrary to your wishes"
[^evil]: crito 49b "is not wrongdoing inevitably an evil and a disgrace to the wrongdoer?"
[^requite]: crito 49b "we ought not even to requite wrong with wrong, as the world thinks"
[^harm]: crito 49c "doing evil to people is the same thing as wronging them"
[^believe]: crito 49d "agree to something you do not believe"
[^few]: crito 49d "there are few who believe or ever will believe this"
[^common]: crito 49d "have no common ground of discussion"
