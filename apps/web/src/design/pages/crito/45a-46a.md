## What’s happening {#happening}

Crito presses every point he has. The risk is his friends’ to run,[^risk] and the money is ready: Crito’s own is enough,[^enough] and Simmias of Thebes has brought funds for this very purpose, with Cebes and many others ready too.[^simmias] Friends in Thessaly would protect him.[^thessaly] Then Crito goes further: staying is not even right. It betrays Socrates himself,[^betray] leaves his sons to the lot of orphans,[^orphans] and takes the lazy way for a man who has talked all his life of virtue.[^lazy] People will think his friends cowards.[^cowardice] It must all be done tonight.[^tonight]

[^risk]: crito 45a "it is right for us to run this risk, and even greater risk than this"
[^enough]: crito 45b "have my money at your command, which is enough, I fancy"
[^simmias]: crito 45b "Simmias of Thebes, has brought for this especial purpose sufficient funds; and Cebes also and very many others are ready"
[^thessaly]: crito 45c "I have friends there who will make much of you and will protect you"
[^betray]: crito 45c "betraying yourself when you might save yourself"
[^orphans]: crito 45d "such treatment as generally comes to orphans in their destitution"
[^lazy]: crito 45d "you seem to me to be choosing the laziest way"
[^cowardice]: crito 45e "conducted with a sort of cowardice on our part"
[^tonight]: crito 46a "all this must be done in the coming night"
