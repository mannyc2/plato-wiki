## What’s happening {#happening}

The Laws speak as his parents. Through them his father married his mother,[^forth] and through them he was raised and educated. So he is their offspring and their slave,[^slave] and they are not equals: a son may not strike back at his father.[^equality] His country deserves more reverence than his mother and father.[^precious] He must persuade her that she is wrong, or do what she commands,[^persuade] and violence against her is worse than violence against a parent.[^impious] Crito agrees that the Laws speak the truth.[^truth]

[^forth]: crito 50d "Is it not through us that your father married your mother and begat you?"
[^slave]: crito 50e "you were not our offspring and our slave"
[^equality]: crito 50e "There was no such equality of right between you and your father"
[^precious]: crito 51a "your country is more precious and more to be revered"
[^persuade]: crito 51b "ought either to convince her by persuasion or to do whatever she commands"
[^impious]: crito 51c "much more impious to use it against your country"
[^truth]: crito 51c "I think they do."
