## What’s happening {#happening}

Nor, the Laws say, would escape help his sons. He could make them exiles in Thessaly,[^exiles] or leave them in Athens, where his friends will care for them whether he is abroad or dead.[^care] He should care for nothing, not children nor life itself, more than for what is right.[^right] If he dies now, he dies wronged not by the laws but by men.[^men] If he escapes, repaying wrong with wrong, their brothers, the laws of the underworld, will not receive him kindly.[^hades] They end by telling him not to let Crito persuade him, but to take their advice.[^advice]

[^exiles]: crito 54a "making exiles of them"
[^care]: crito 54a "Will they care for them if you go away to Thessaly and not if you go away to the dwellings of the dead?"
[^right]: crito 54b "Care neither for your children nor for life nor for anything else more than for the right"
[^men]: crito 54b-c "you will go away wronged, if you do go away, not by us, the laws, but by men"
[^hades]: crito 54c "our brothers, the laws in Hades’ realm, will not receive you graciously"
[^advice]: crito 54c "Do not let Crito persuade you to do"
