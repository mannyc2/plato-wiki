## What’s happening {#happening}

No one, the Laws say, forced or tricked him into the agreement,[^forced] and he had seventy years in which to leave.[^seventy] He never chose Sparta (Lacedaemon) or Crete, though he is always praising their laws.[^sparta] He left Athens less often than the lame and the blind.[^lame] Who could be pleased with a city and not with its laws?[^apart] If he keeps his agreement now, they end, he will not make himself ridiculous.[^ridiculous]

[^forced]: crito 52e "you were not led into them by compulsion or fraud"
[^seventy]: crito 52e "had seventy years, in which you could have gone away"
[^sparta]: crito 52e "you preferred neither Lacedaemon nor Crete, which you are always saying are well governed"
[^lame]: crito 53a "you went away from this city less than the lame and the blind and the other cripples"
[^apart]: crito 53a "who would be pleased with a city apart from its laws?"
[^ridiculous]: crito 53a "you will not make yourself ridiculous by going away from the city"
