## What’s happening {#happening}

The Laws have finished, and Socrates speaks as himself again.[^hear] Their words ring in him the way the flutes ring for Cybele’s worshippers, so loudly that he can hear nothing else.[^ring] As things seem to him now, he says, arguing against them would be in vain; still, Crito may speak if he thinks he can achieve anything.[^vain] Crito has nothing to say.[^nothing] So Socrates will stay: let them act this way, he says, since this is the way the god leads.[^god]
