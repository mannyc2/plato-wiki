# Socrates

Seventy years old and before a court for the first time,[^seventy] he answers the charge that he corrupts the youth and does not believe in the gods the state believes in, but in other new spiritual beings.[^charge] He speaks first in his defence,[^defence] then after the verdict to propose a penalty,[^penalty] and last, sentenced to death,[^sentenced] to those who condemned him and to those who voted to acquit him.[^acquit]

## Where we find him {#scene}

:::passage{ref="apology 17c-d" from="I urgently beg" to="manner of speech here." lit="seventy years old"}
Opening his defence, he asks the jury to bear with him if he speaks as he does in the market place.
:::

:::passage{ref="apology 39e-40a" from="But with those who voted" to="while there is time." lit="Wait with me so long, my friends"}
Sentenced to death, he stays to talk with those who voted to acquit him.
:::

## How he argues {#moves}

:::moves
1. He tests the god’s answer by questioning men reputed wise, and finds himself wiser only in this: what he does not know, he does not think he knows.[^oracle]
2. He questions his accuser before the jury until Meletus, as it appears to Socrates, contradicts himself.[^contradict]
3. No one knows whether death is not the greatest of blessings; to fear it is to think one knows what one does not.[^death]
4. He will obey the god rather than the jury, and never give up philosophy while he lives.[^obey]
5. If they kill him, he says, they will harm themselves more than him.[^harm]
6. Asked to propose a penalty, he first proposes what he says he deserves: maintenance in the prytaneum.[^prytaneum]
7. Death, he says, is either like a dreamless sleep or a migration of the soul, and there is good reason to hope it is a good thing.[^hope]
:::

## Also in the Apology {#cast}

:::people
- meletus: His accuser, whom he calls up to answer questions before the jury.[^meletus]
- crito: Of his own age and deme, present at the trial,[^crito] and among those who urge him to propose a fine of thirty minas and offer to stand surety for it.[^surety]
