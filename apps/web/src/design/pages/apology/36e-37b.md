## What’s happening {#happening}

Still speaking after the verdict, Socrates says that if he must propose a penalty in keeping with his deserts, he proposes maintenance in the prytaneum.[^maintenance] Some may think this bravado, he says, but it is not:[^bravado] he is convinced he never intentionally wronged anyone,[^never] and cannot convince the jury of it because they have talked together only a little while.[^little] If Athens had a law, as some others do, that capital cases take several days, not one, he believes they would be convinced.[^days] Convinced, he says, that he never wronged anyone, he will not wrong himself by saying he deserves anything bad.[^himself] Why should he, for fear of the penalty Meletus proposes, which he says he does not know to be good or evil,[^know] choose instead something he knows to be an evil?[^evil]

[^bravado]: apology 37a "I am speaking in a spirit of bravado; but that is not the case"

[^little]: apology 37a "we have conversed with each other only a little while"
[^days]: apology 37b "that capital cases should not be decided in one day, but only after several days, you would be convinced"
[^himself]: apology 37b "Since, then, I am convinced that I never wronged any one, I am certainly not going to wrong myself"
[^know]: apology 37b "about which I say that I do not know whether it is a good thing or an evil"
[^evil]: apology 37b "Shall I choose instead of that something which I know to be an evil?"
