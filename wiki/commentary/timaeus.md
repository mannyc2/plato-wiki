# Timaeus Commentary

Directly curated teaching material anchored to the complete Greek dialogue. The
section spine preserves the dialogue's own uncertainty about cosmological
accounts: the account is presented as likely and fitting, not as settled doctrine.

body: Read this closing unit as a transfer of method. Timaeus first ranks bodily motions and recommends regimen over unnecessary drugging; he then applies exercise and proportion to the three kinds of soul.
