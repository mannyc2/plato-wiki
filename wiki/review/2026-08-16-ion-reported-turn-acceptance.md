# Ion reported-turn cohort acceptance

**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Scope:** `turn_ion_0089`, `turn_ion_0120`
**Canonical source:** `raw/plato/greek/ion.txt` (`0528d703134b2f59f3db31bad84bafb320e5022e1d7660cf64d72034cb29c93d`)

## Decision

Accepted the complete two-turn Ion cohort in `wiki/voices/ion.md` after direct
Greek review.

- `turn_ion_0089` keeps Ion as the printed frame and assigns its bounded
  `{quote}` recitation to Nestor, whom Socrates names in the immediately
  preceding introducing formula `λέγει Νέστωρ`.
- `turn_ion_0120` keeps Socrates as the printed frame and assigns the
  `{q}`-bounded counterfactual question to Ion: `σοῦ ἐρομένου, εἰ ἔροιό με`
  grammatically makes the present interlocutor the staged questioner.
- The Theoclymenus and Iliad verse blocks later in `turn_ion_0120` stay out of
  the cohort because Socrates cites them inside his own argument; the scope
  census already reviewed that distinction.

The three dialogue-local identifiers `ΣΩ.`, `ΙΩΝ.`, and `ΝΕΣ.` were added to
`derived/plato/voices/sigla.toml` only to name these source-licensed owners.
No cutover or voice join is authorized by this acceptance.
