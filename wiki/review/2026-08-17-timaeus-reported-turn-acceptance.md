## Decision

Accept the complete twenty-eight-record cohort after direct Greek review and an
independent Greek-only second read.

- The 21a–25e frames retain `ΚΡ.` while the bounded report is represented as
  `ΚΡΙΠ.`; `τὰ μὲν δὴ ῥηθέντα ... ὑπὸ τοῦ παλαιοῦ Κριτίου` closes that report.
  The Egyptian-priest and Solon pieces remain nested beneath it. Bare q pieces
  without a terminal owner remain unresolved rather than inheriting a speaker.
- The doubled `{q}` markup in 0039–0041 records the mechanically split
  continuation of the same priest speech, bounded by `τὸν οὖν ἱερέα φάναι` and
  the later `οὓς ἔλεγεν ὁ ἱερεύς`.
- At 41a–d, `ὁ τόδε τὸ πᾶν γεννήσας` licenses the one direct address. The
  following `εἶπεν αὐταῖς, ὅτι` plus optatives in 0063 remains indirect and out
  of scope.

The dialogue-local identifiers `ΚΡ.`, `ΤΙ.`, `ΚΡΙΠ.`, `ΣΟΛ.`, `ΙΕΡ.`, and
`ΔΗΜ.` were added only for these source-licensed frames and terminal owners. No
cutover or voice join is authorized by this acceptance.
