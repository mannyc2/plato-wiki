## Decision

Accept the complete thirty-one-record cohort after direct Greek review.

- The fourteen printed `ΣΩ.` frames preserve the continuous 236d–249c oration
  as `ΑΣΠ.`. Its opening feminine report `ἔλεγε ... ἀρξαμένη λέγειν` and its
  closing `Ἀσπασίας τῆς Μιλησίας ἐστίν` are the source brackets; re-set printed
  sigla at page boundaries do not transfer the oration to Socrates.
- At 246d–248d, the fathers' address is a nested direct collective: `πατέρες`
  and `ἐκεῖνοι` are plural. Its three source-bounded segments remain unresolved
  rather than receiving a fictional singular owner.
- The indirect Darius material in 0029, the current orator's 0032 recap, and
  the 0036 proverb remain inside their enclosing Aspasia record, as the scope
  census already distinguishes them from another reported owner.

Only `ΣΩ.` and `ΑΣΠ.` were added to the Menexenus voice registry for the
source-licensed frame and the bounded oration. No cutover or voice join is
authorized by this acceptance.
