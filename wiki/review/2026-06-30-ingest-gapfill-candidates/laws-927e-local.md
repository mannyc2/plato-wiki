  The orphan legislation distinguishes daily way of life from civic treatment. The Athenian says special guardianship laws would make sense if orphan life needed private practices very different from non-orphan life, but in this city orphanhood is not very different from life under paternal law in such matters; what cannot be equalized is its treatment through honors, dishonors, and forms of care.
