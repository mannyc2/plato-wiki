There is no chariot-rearer and no reasonable ambition for chariots, so to set chariot contestants would show no sense. By setting prizes for single horses, foals, intermediate horses, and full-grown horses, they give horsemanship play according to the nature of the country. The law gives common judgment of all these races and armed dismounting contests to tribe-commanders and cavalry-commanders. It would not be right to establish unarmed contests either in gymnastic events or here. A Cretan mounted archer or javelin-thrower is not useless, so there should be rivalry and contest for these as play. For females, the law should not force participation by commands, but if earlier education has become habit and nature permits girls or maidens to share without distress, they should be allowed and not blamed."
