A better lawgiver divides it in two: simple ignorance is the cause of light errors, while double ignorance occurs when someone is held not only by ignorance but by the opinion of wisdom, as if he knew completely what he does not know at all. With strength and force this causes great and unmusical errors; with weakness it causes errors of children and elders, for which the laws should be gentlest and most full of pardon."

When this third itself is split, the three become five kinds, and laws differing from one another must be set for the five kinds in two classes. The classes are actions done through violent and manifest acts, and acts done secretly with darkness and deception; sometimes a deed is done through both, and in such cases laws would be harshest if they had their fitting share."
