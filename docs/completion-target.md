# v1.0 Completion Target

> Historical v1 target. Its neutral, source-grounded vocabulary rule remains
> applicable, while its label-keyed ontology and completion claims are
> superseded by `docs/ontology-vnext.md` and the snapshot-bound audit package.

Ratified per the v1 completion target. This document defines the single "done" version of
the Plato observation wiki. When every gate below is checked and the
decision line is signed, the project is defensibly complete: further work is
optional extension, not unfinished scope.

Vocabulary rule for everything this target covers: all artifacts, prompts,
reports, page names, and record fields use neutral, source-grounded,
structural vocabulary. No downstream artifact names or targets an
interpretive school or a hidden-meaning thesis. Patterns are surfaced as
records, metrics, comparisons, and absence views; readers draw their own
conclusions.

## What v1.0 is

A self-contained, regenerable knowledge base over 27 Greek Plato dialogues:

1. an observation corpus with verifiable spans, review statuses, and
   provenance (complete at `ca12008`);
2. a label layer whose reusable core and singleton tail are both *measured
   and adjudicated*, not merely frozen;
3. deterministic structural layers: stephanus, speaker turns, tokens,
   anchors, metrics, observation-turn joins;
4. an evidence layer of pattern dossiers (instances, cross-dialogue
   presence/absence, co-occurrence, counterevidence) built from accepted
   records only;
5. a claim layer recording what is asserted, challenged, conceded, revised,
   withdrawn, or left standing, per dialogue, with review statuses;
6. a relation layer recording contradictions, tensions, revisions, and
   restatements between reviewed claims, separating refutation-resolved
   from left-standing cases;
7. a static frontend that renders all of the above with stable permalinks,
   filters, comparison and absence views, and a weak-spots dashboard;
8. a completion report and known-limits document.

## Gates

Ontology (plans 039-040):
- [ ] G1: `wiki/label-quality.md` exists, regenerated byte-stable, and
      reports: reuse-mass shares, per-dialogue cross-dialogue-label
      participation, disposition coverage against all merge maps, per-family
      singleton profiles, label-name length distribution.
- [ ] G2: a stratified singleton adjudication sample (target >= 300 labels)
      is reviewed under `docs/label-normalization-standards.md`, with
      per-label dispositions recorded in a merge-map-format JSON artifact
      and composition estimates published.
- [ ] G3: the singleton tail is dispositioned by one of: (a) measured
      composition accepted in a signed memo (estimated same-function
      mergeable share and passage-summary share within the bounds the memo
      sets); (b) a targeted, named-family normalization pass licensed by
      that memo and executed through `labels plan/validate/apply`; or (c) a
      combination. "Unmeasured" is not an accepted state.
- [ ] G4: per-dialogue participation in cross-dialogue labels is published;
      any dialogue below the memo-ratified floor is explicitly listed in
      the known-limits document.

Evidence layer (plans 041-043):
- [ ] E1: every accepted cross-dialogue label has a dossier under
      `wiki/dossiers/` with instance table, 27-dialogue presence/absence

## Stop conditions in force until v1.0

- Label layer: frozen per the 2026-07-03 decision. The only permitted label
  changes are a targeted pass licensed by the singleton-adjudication memo for named
  same-function families, executed via `labels plan/validate/apply`.
- No new global normalization passes. No renaming outside the licensed pass.
- No new derived-data generators beyond: observation-turn joins (041),
  dossiers (041), claim ledgers (042), relation records (043), and the
  quality/sample reports (039). Particle metrics, hiatus/clausula metrics,
  frame/closure skeletons, dialogue manifest, speaker directory, and
  reported-speech segmentation are post-v1.0.
- LLM corpus writes are limited to: the adjudication sample review (040),
  claim extraction and review (042), relation adjudication (043), and
  repair of validation failures. No new observation ingest passes.
- Frontend page inventory is fixed by F2. New page types are post-v1.0.
- Agent-visible tool surface stays as-is; the BACKLOG P8 tools
  (`wiki_derived_read`, `wiki_anchor_search`, `wiki_turn_metrics`) remain
  deferred. Extraction passes receive derived context composed
  deterministically into prompts by the harness instead.
